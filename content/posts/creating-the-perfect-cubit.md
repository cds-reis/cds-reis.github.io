Hello. Today I am in the mood to talk about the Cubit class, from the [bloc](https://bloclibrary.dev/) package. More specifically, I want to talk about how you should design your cubit classes, and how you should consider the architecture of your application when designing your cubit classes.

Note that this isn't a Bloc/Cubit tutorial. Some understanding and experience of Flutter and the package are assumed with this post. This post is intended to be a guide on how to design your cubit classes, and not a tutorial on how to use the package.

Also worth noting that all the advice I give for designing Cubits also apply for the more general Bloc class. I am using Cubit sicne it is more straightforward, and personally, my preference.
